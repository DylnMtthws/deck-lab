# Research Assistant augmentation: October 20, 2026

The implementation adds request-specific evidence obligations, sentence-level claim review, one corrective retrieval pass, scalable offline vector storage, attributable source ingestion, finite mechanical traces, and coverage diagnostics. None of these receipts certifies strategic strength or an unlimited loop.

## Selection and review

`build_strategic_obligations` records exact source spans/hashes, conditions, and established/conditional/unknown/contradicted propositions. Omitted context differs from support not found. Fixed resource arithmetic requires a complete recognized clause. Triggered consumption and postposed conditions remain relevant. Deterministic economics ordering applies only to explicit draw-only requests; other roles and mixed requests retain existing ranking. The selector receives structured obligations for comparison, including uncertainty.

Claim review retains exact sentence spans and their full candidate paragraph. A supported conditional implication does not require proof that its premise will occur. Voluntarily initiating an activated ability differs from mandatory draw instructions during resolution. Corrections belong to a particular reviewed span. Rendering reconstructs accepted fragments so appended or interleaved unreviewed text cannot inherit a verdict. The existing sidebar partial-finding path displays checked fragments while remaining work is pending.

A corrective pass identifies unresolved propositions, performs at most two distinct local lookups, and appends only complete attributed official evidence. New evidence permits one additional batch reviewing affected claim IDs. It preserves unaffected patches and original text bindings; subset review cannot upgrade whole-request fulfillment. Future board states, strategic superiority, survival and infinite loops are outside this lookup contract.

Output replacement requires a recognizable owned index. Queries encode once and nominate a deterministic global top 24 across caller-eligible identities. Current source, projection, model, dimension and vector hashes guard compatibility; malformed rows abstain and can be re-encoded. Legacy JSON remains supported.

Canonical evidence and legacy search projections remain separate. Refresh receipts distinguish coverage, source publication, fetch/import times and successful local publication. Missing upstream dates remain unknown.
